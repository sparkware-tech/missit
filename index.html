About

Not just a teacher.
A passionate IT educator.

Shammi is a qualified IT educator holding a BSc IT (Honours) from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura — one of Sri Lanka's leading universities for technology education.

With a passion for making IT accessible to every student regardless of language, she founded Miss IT to bridge the gap between dense syllabus content and real understanding.

  • Experienced teaching in both English and Sinhala medium
  • Former instructor at the Inventors' Society — Dutugamunu Maha Vidyalaya
  • BSc IT (Honours) — University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka
  • Active IT educator on social media
