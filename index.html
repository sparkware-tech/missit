Teaching Philosophy

"I want my students to walk into their IT exam with confidence — not just knowledge, but the ability to think, apply, and succeed. Whether you learn best in English or Sinhala, Miss IT has a class for you."— Shammi, Miss IT

The Problem

Most IT resources in Sri Lanka are only in English, leaving Sinhala-medium students at a disadvantage. Textbooks are often dense and disconnected from real-world application.

The Mission

Miss IT exists to give every Grade 6–11 student equal access to quality IT guidance — exam prep, practical skills, and digital literacy in their own language.

The Approach

Real-life examples, structured revision, and a supportive learning environment. Complex topics become simple when explained the right way in the right language.

Programming Basics

What is an Algorithm?

An algorithm is a step-by-step set of instructions to solve a problem — like a recipe for the computer to follow.

Hardware

RAM vs ROM

RAM stores temporary data while your computer runs. ROM stores permanent data that never changes, even when powered off.

Internet Safety

Strong Password Rules

Use 8+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Never use the same password on two different websites!

Exam Tips

Top 5 Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl+C (Copy), Ctrl+V (Paste), Ctrl+Z (Undo), Ctrl+S (Save), Ctrl+P (Print). These appear on almost every exam!

Programming Basics

What is a Variable?

A variable is like a labelled box that stores a value in a program. You can change what's inside the box at any time.

Hardware

CPU vs GPU

The CPU is the brain of the computer — it handles all general processing. The GPU is specialized for graphics and display.

Exam Tips

Read Questions Twice

In ICT exams, read each question twice before answering. Many marks are lost from misreading what is actually being asked.

Internet Safety

Phishing Attacks

If an email or message asks for your personal details or password, it is likely a phishing attempt. Never click suspicious links!

Programming Basics

What is a Loop?

A loop repeats a set of instructions multiple times. For example: printing numbers 1 to 100 without writing 100 lines of code.
